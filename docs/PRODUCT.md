# Product brief

## Category and positioning

Mastline is vertical SaaS for celebrity and news photography. It is both a system of record and a system of action: workflow management, asset intelligence, sales operations, revenue tracking, archive activation, and rights oversight designed around news urgency.

The primary user is the photographer or small agency operating the business. This is not a publisher portal and should not be framed as a two-sided marketplace. A direct-sale channel may exist inside the product, but the business OS remains the center of gravity.

## Problem

The image moves fast; the business record does not. Critical facts scatter across texts and tips, maps, notes, cards, editing tools, agency portals, spreadsheets, email, invoices, and memory. This creates missed opportunities, slower dispatch, invisible receivables, dormant archives, and unresolved usage.

## Product areas

1. **Command & opportunity:** Work Queue and News Monitor
2. **Shoot & field operations:** Create Shoot and Shoot Workspace
3. **Dispatch & sales:** Dispatch Review and Submission Record
4. **Asset, rights & revenue:** Asset Record, Rights Matches, Revenue & Payments, Archive

## Initial wedge

Shoot workflow, dispatch, submission tracking, asset records, and payments. The first promise is not that Mastline sees every opportunity; it is that the operator no longer loses the commercial thread after capturing the image.

## Phase strategy

### Phase 1 — Operator OS

Reliable assignment-to-payment path: work queue, shoots, intake, selects, captions, metadata checks, dispatch, submission records, archive, and payments.

### Phase 2 — Revenue intelligence

News-to-archive matching, buyer-fit recommendations, statement import/reconciliation, rights-match evidence and triage, splits, expenses, and net profit.

### Phase 3 — Scale and control

Advanced roles, approvals, workload, agency reporting, outlet templates, integrations, retention, governance, and audit/export controls.

## Success measures

| Dimension | Measures |
| --- | --- |
| Speed | Capture-to-first-dispatch; metadata pass rate; weekly full-loop completion |
| Earnings | Submission-to-sale rate; revenue/margin per shoot; archive share of revenue |
| Certainty | Sale-to-payment days; aging receivables; verified rights matches/recovery |

North star: the share of active operators who complete the whole commercial loop weekly. Passive storage is not success.

## Final pricing architecture

- **Solo:** $49/month billed annually or $59 monthly; 1 photographer, core workflow, revenue reporting, and 250 GB.
- **Pro:** $99/month billed annually or $119 monthly; Solo plus live news monitoring, archive-to-story matching, rights/usage monitoring, advanced revenue analytics, and 1 TB.
- **Studio:** $279/month billed annually or $339 monthly; Pro plus up to 5 people, dispatch/review queues, roles/approvals, team revenue allocation, and 5 TB shared.
- **Agency:** custom pricing for larger organizations, migration, API/integrations, custom permissions, priority support, and flexible storage.

Paid plans may use the optional Mastline Sales Engine. The photographer receives 70% and Mastline receives 30% only when Mastline generates the license. Subscription value should stand on its own; do not frame the company as a two-sided marketplace.

## Trust model

Mastline holds sensitive media, locations, buyer relationships, financial records, and potential evidence. Provenance, access control, encryption, backups, retention, export, human review, source protection, and labeled suggestions are product architecture—not administrative cleanup.
